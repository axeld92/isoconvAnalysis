# ICTAC-aligned assessment of TG kinetics

Generated by `scripts/audit_ictac_tg.jl`. This assessment covers the declared experiments and apparent activation energy. The accompanying [TOML](audits/ictac_tg_assessment.toml) retains the numerical settings, source hashes, conversion windows, and per-point diagnostics.

This assessment follows the ICTAC recommendations on [2011 computation](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tca.2011.03.034), [2014 data collection](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tca.2014.05.036), [2020 multistep kinetics](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tca.2020.178597), and [2023 decomposition](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tca.2022.179384).

The approximately tenfold ramp spread is advisory. A reported central energy range describes the estimates over α = 0.10–0.90. The 2020 paper's 10–20% guide depends on uncertainty. Scenario shifts are sensitivity evidence and the fixed ramp probe is a diagnostic at one declared conversion. Each status applies to the individual check.
