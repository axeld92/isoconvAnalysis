# M9 corrected Julia-versus-legacy comparison

Generated by `scripts/audit_legacy_comparison.jl` on 2026-11-12. The audit compares preserved legacy evidence with the corrected Julia workflow; it does not treat every historical workspace value as ground truth.

## Decision

Exact numerical parity is required and achieved at the raw dynamic import boundary: 20/20 canonical variables are exactly equal to `St01`-`St20`, including the trailing NaN rows. Beyond that boundary, the Julia implementation preserves scientific method families while intentionally correcting identified defects, making assumptions explicit, and applying synthetic or held-out validation.

The file named `2021marcorrected.mat` is not a numerical preprocessing oracle. All 20/20 arrays are exact contiguous copies of raw columns 1-4, with start temperatures 110.006-110.105 C and end temperatures 794.430-798.739 C. It contains no stored normalized conversion, smoothed derivative, fit, or uncertainty result.

The 12 saved result/publication workspaces have no recorded producing command, environment, script revision, or unambiguous variable lineage in the archive. They remain qualitative workflow evidence and are excluded from release acceptance thresholds.

## Method-level comparison

| Milestone | Area | Legacy behavior | Julia behavior | Status | Conclusion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| M2 | Raw data ingestion | Direct MAT variables and St01-St20 aliases | Checksum-verified, explicitly named MAT variables with preserved invalid rows | `exact` | All 20 configured dynamic arrays are exactly equal to their aliases, including NaNs. |
| M3 | Preprocessing | Row endpoints, Savitzky-Golay smoothing, diff_alpha, and two low-pass calls | Explicit 150-700 C references, local-polynomial smoothing, diagnostics, and eight sensitivity profiles | `intentional_deviation` | The scientific conversion-and-derivative objective is preserved; numerical parity is neither available nor accepted. |
| M4 | Linear isoconversional methods | Four-run Friedman, KAS, FWO, and Starink scripts | Named multi-experiment methods with unit checks, first-crossing interpolation, and two-sided slope intervals | `corrected_equations` | Method families are preserved, while known Friedman and temperature-handling defects are corrected. |
| M4 | Advanced Vyazovkin method | Optimization of distance from a hard-coded objective value of 12 | Bounded minimization of the true pairwise objective with Fisher intervals | `corrected_equations` | The method concept is preserved; the corrected objective is expected to produce different values. |
| M5 | Fraser-Suzuki deconvolution | Global/workspace fits, complex-to-zero masking, and sequentially tightened bounds | Stable real-valued peak definition, independent deterministic multistart fits, and structural model selection | `intentional_deviation` | Curve decomposition is preserved, but order dependence and invalid-domain masking are removed. |
| M6 | Kinetic triplets and reaction models | Normal-equation regressions with duplicated or confounded intercept terms | Single-intercept QR fits, a named model registry, diagnostics, and held-out-rate gates | `intentional_deviation` | The candidate equations are preserved as empirical models; legacy coefficients are not valid parameter oracles. |
| M7 | Dynamic and ramp-and-hold prediction | Workspace-dependent simulations with hard-coded runs and no frozen predictive gate | Bounded integration, calibration-only parameters, and 15 held-out ramp-and-hold programs | `not_numerically_comparable` | Both evaluated Julia formulations are rejected; matching legacy plots would not establish prediction quality. |
| M8 | Mixture additivity | Observed-minus-additive conversion/rate curves after legacy preprocessing | Matched-rate measured curves, three channels, signed integrals, and preprocessing envelopes | `concept_preserved` | The sign convention and additive concept agree, but corrected preprocessing and expanded diagnostics preclude pointwise parity. |

## Intentional deviations

| ID | Area | Legacy evidence | Julia treatment | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `LD01` | Data schema | Raw arrays use mass in column 4; derived five-column workspaces use column 3. | Select schemas by catalog source and named columns, never by column count alone. | Prevents silent interpretation of DSC or derivative values as mass. |
| `LD02` | Corrected workspace | Every 2021marcorrected array is an exact contiguous copy of raw columns 1-4 beginning near 110 C. | Classify it as a cropped raw reference, not processed ground truth. | The artifact contains no normalized conversion or smoothed derivative to reproduce. |
| `LD03` | Preprocessing | cleandata.m normalizes with retained endpoint rows and applies undocumented smoothing and low-pass choices. | Estimate exact-temperature reference masses robustly and expose smoothing and endpoint sensitivity. | Makes the conversion coordinate and derivative bias explicit and testable. |
| `LD04` | Friedman regression | friedman.m has incorrect heating-rate parentheses and interpolates Temp_4 instead of da_4. | Use measured heating rate and the conversion derivative for every experiment. | Never preserve known algebraic or variable-selection defects for parity. |
| `LD05` | Isoconversional generality | Several scripts accept exactly four runs and mix Celsius-to-kelvin conversion conventions. | Accept arbitrary valid experiment groups and enforce kelvin at the typed boundary. | Removes fixed-design and double-conversion failure modes. |
| `LD06` | Advanced Vyazovkin objective | Vyazovkin.m minimizes abs(phiofe2(...)-12), where 12 assumes four equal pair terms. | Minimize the true pairwise objective with bounded search and method-specific intervals. | The optimum must not depend on a hard-coded experiment-count target. |
| `LD07` | Fraser-Suzuki domain | deconvolve.m replaces complex-valued evaluations with zero. | Use a stable Gaussian limit and a continuous real-valued zero outside the logarithm domain. | Avoids silently accepting invalid intermediate arithmetic. |
| `LD08` | Deconvolution reproducibility | deconvolve.m hard-codes St05; deconvolution4.m tightens bounds from each preceding fit. | Fit each named experiment independently with deterministic multistart optimization. | Removes hidden workspace inputs and experiment-order dependence. |
| `LD09` | Peak accounting and interpretation | deconvolve4.m omits peak 4 from denominators and assigns its fraction from peak 3. | Integrate every selected peak and keep peaks ordered but chemically unnamed. | Corrects area fractions without inventing component identity. |
| `LD10` | Kinetic regression | kineticanalysis.m duplicates the constant column and solves least squares through normal equations. | Fit one ln(A/min^-1) intercept using QR and retain rank, conditioning, and predictive diagnostics. | Restores parameter identifiability and numerical stability. |
| `LD11` | Reaction-model intervals | sblin.m retains amplitude/prefactor confounding; sbrlin.m reports observation-sized prediction widths as coefficient intervals. | Separate coefficient intervals, residual diagnostics, model criteria, and cross-rate prediction. | Avoids overstating parameter certainty from densely sampled derivative points. |
| `LD12` | Compensation and simulation | compeffects.m includes a wrong Ginstling-Brounshtein expression; the simulation path uses globals and hard-coded St16. | Keep compensation diagnostic and simulate only frozen, provenance-carrying, bounded formulations. | Prevents circular parameter substitution and nonphysical hidden-state propagation. |
| `LD13` | Mixture interaction | synergy.m uses observed-minus-additive differences after legacy preprocessing. | Preserve the sign convention while reporting mass, conversion, rate, signed areas, and preprocessing scenarios. | Retains the scientific comparison without claiming a named chemical synergy mechanism. |

## Evidence tiers and provenance

The machine record at `docs/audits/m9_legacy_comparison.toml` stores a SHA-256 hash for every MAT artifact used in this comparison. The tiers have different evidentiary force:

- `canonical_raw` and `raw_alias`: exact numerical comparison is authoritative.
- `cropped_raw_reference`: authoritative only for the exact raw subset it contains.
- `held_out_raw`: authoritative measured input, reserved for prediction validation.
- `derived_workspace`: schema and workflow context; transformation lineage is incomplete.
- `saved_result_workspace` and `publication_workspace`: snapshot context only, not numerical acceptance oracles.
The method comparison preserves findings from the historical script review. The original MATLAB source is stored separately.

## Release consequence

The M9 legacy comparison and intentional-deviation record are complete. A future difference in raw alias equality is a regression. A difference in corrected preprocessing, fitted activation energies, peak parameters, reaction-model coefficients, or simulations is not by itself a regression; those results are governed by the Julia equations, diagnostics, frozen configurations, synthetic tests, and predictive gates documented in M3-M8.

Subsequent release work is tracked in PROJECT_STATUS.md; this audit remains the frozen evidence for the legacy-comparison and intentional-deviation portion of M9.
